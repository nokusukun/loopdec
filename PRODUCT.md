# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Musicians, DJs, producers, and media enthusiasts who need to loop YouTube content. They open LoopDec when they want to load multiple YouTube clips and loop them, whether practicing along to a performance, previewing samples, or just keeping clips running in the background. They expect a tool that respects their time: paste a link, set a loop, move on.

## Product Purpose

LoopDec downloads YouTube clips via yt-dlp and presents them as a multi-panel looper. Each clip can be independently controlled (enable/disable, custom loop sections) with a master transport for global play/stop. It exists because YouTube's native looping is single-clip, buried in menus, and surrounded by distractions. Success looks like: paste a URL, clip is playing in under 10 seconds, loop points are set in two clicks.

## Brand Personality

Dark, creative, bold. LoopDec feels like a piece of studio equipment: confident in what it does, zero ornamentation, every element earns its space. The interface communicates competence without pretension.

## Anti-references

- YouTube and streaming platform UIs: no recommendations, no social chrome, no algorithmic suggestions, no bloated player controls
- Generic SaaS dashboards: no cards-in-a-grid layouts, no sidebar navigation, no metric widgets, no onboarding wizards

## Design Principles

1. **Immediate utility** - Every interaction gets the user closer to hearing their loop. No setup flows, no configuration screens.
2. **Session view, not page view** - Clips are live objects in a session, not items in a list. The layout should feel like an Ableton session view: everything visible, everything controllable.
3. **Density without clutter** - Show more, chrome less. Controls are compact and precise. Whitespace is intentional, not filler.
4. **Transport-native** - Play, stop, loop are first-class citizens. Transport controls feel physical and immediate, not buried in menus.
5. **Respect the content** - The video is the point. UI gets out of the way of the media.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort accessibility: good contrast ratios, keyboard-navigable controls, visible focus states. No strict WCAG compliance target, but the dark theme should maintain readable contrast throughout.
